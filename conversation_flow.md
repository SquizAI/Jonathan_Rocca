# AI Chatbot Conversation Flow

## Initial Outreach

### First Contact
- **Template**: "Hi, I'm [Agent Name] with [Company]. I noticed you own the property at [Address]. We have an investor interested in purchasing properties in your area. Would you be open to discussing a potential offer?"
- **Variables**: Agent Name, Company, Address
- **Tone**: Professional, direct but conversational
- **Goal**: Establish contact and gauge initial interest

### Response Handling
- **If No Response**: Follow up in 24 hours with slight variation
- **If Negative**: Thank them for their time, flag for future follow-up in 6-8 weeks
- **If Question About Identity**: Provide brief professional background
- **If Initial Interest**: Move to qualification phase

## Qualification Phase

### Property Status Questions
- "Is this property your primary residence or an investment property?"
- "How long have you owned the property?"
- "Are there any current tenants in the property?"
- "Have you considered selling in the past year?"

### Financial Qualification
- "What do you think your property is currently worth?"
- "Are there any mortgages or liens on the property?"
- "Would you be interested in a 1031 exchange option?"
- "What timeframe would you be looking at for a potential sale?"

### Interest Level Assessment
- **Low Interest Indicators**: Vague responses, long delays, price expectations far above market
- **Medium Interest Indicators**: Asking about process, timeline questions, comparing to other options
- **High Interest Indicators**: Asking for specific offer details, discussing availability for showings, mentioning urgent needs

## Lead Conversion

### Hand-Raiser Identification
- **Trigger Phrases**:
  - "Yes, I would be interested in an offer"
  - "What kind of price are you thinking?"
  - "When could we meet to discuss?"
  - "How soon could you close?"
  - "I've been thinking about selling"

### Human Handoff Process
- Alert dashboard with conversation transcript
- Flag specific high-interest indicators
- Categorize lead priority (Hot, Warm, Cold)
- Recommend next action for human follow-up

## Nurture Sequences

### For "Not Ready" Responses
- 2-week follow-up: "Just checking in about [Address]"
- 6-week follow-up: "We have a new investor looking in your area"
- 3-month follow-up: "Market update for properties like yours"

### For "Maybe Later" Responses
- 1-week follow-up: "Additional information about our buying process"
- 1-month follow-up: "Recent comparable sales in your neighborhood"
- 3-month follow-up: "New financing options for sellers"

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections and Responses
- **"Not interested"**: "No problem. Would it be ok if I reach out in the future if we have a specific buyer looking for a property like yours?"
- **"How did you get my number?"**: "We use public property records to connect with owners. I apologize for the unexpected contact. Would you like more information about our services?"
- **"Is this a scam?"**: "I understand your concern. [Company] is a licensed real estate firm in [State]. You can verify our license at [Website]. Would you feel more comfortable with a formal email introduction?"
- **"I want more money"**: "I understand getting the best value is important. What price range were you hoping for? I'd be happy to see if we can work within your expectations."

## Conversation Analytics

### Data Capture Points

### Optimization Metrics
- Completion rate of conversation flow
- Drop-off points
